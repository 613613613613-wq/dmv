## 1. What the app is

Deal Guard is a software tool that helps you keep track of your own deal terms
during a negotiation. It transcribes speech (when you enable it), compares what
is said against terms you entered, shows short cues, and produces a written
memorandum after the call. It is a productivity aid. It does not negotiate,
advise, or act for you.
