Use this **Sora 2-ready prompt**:

```text
Create a realistic DMV driving lesson video teaching the rule for a 4-way stop.

Length: 40 seconds.
Format: 16:9 horizontal.
Style: realistic, clean, educational, bright daylight, calm suburban road.
Camera: smooth cinematic but simple, with clear overhead views when explaining right-of-way.
Mood: calm, safe, easy for a new driver to understand.
No crashes, no honking, no aggressive driving.

The video should clearly teach:
“4-way stop: first to arrive, first to go.”
“If two cars stop at the same time, the car on the RIGHT has the right of way.”

OPENING — 0:00–0:04
Show a clean suburban 4-way intersection in daylight.
There is a red STOP sign on all four corners.
White stop lines are visible on the road.
Camera begins with a wide front view, then slowly rises into a slight overhead view.

On-screen title:
“4-Way Stop”
Subtitle:
“First to arrive, first to go”

Voiceover:
“At a four-way stop, the basic rule is simple: first to arrive, first to go.”

SCENE 1 — COMPLETE STOP — 0:04–0:08
Show a blue car approaching the stop sign from the bottom of the screen.
The car slows down smoothly and comes to a full stop before the white stop line.
The stop must be obvious: wheels stop completely for a moment.

On-screen text:
“Always come to a complete stop.”

Voiceover:
“Every driver must come to a complete stop before entering the intersection.”

SCENE 2 — FIRST TO ARRIVE GOES FIRST — 0:08–0:17
Keep the overhead view.
The blue car is already stopped.
A yellow car arrives from the left and stops after the blue car.
A gray car arrives from the top and stops after the yellow car.

Add small floating labels:
Blue car: “1st”
Yellow car: “2nd”
Gray car: “3rd”

On-screen text:
“Whoever stops first goes first.”

Voiceover:
“The car that stops first has the right of way and goes first.”

Show a green arrow above the blue car.
The blue car drives straight through the intersection safely.
The yellow and gray cars stay stopped.

On-screen text:
“Blue stopped first → Blue goes first”

Voiceover:
“Here, the blue car stopped first, so the blue car goes first.”

SCENE 3 — NEXT CAR GOES — 0:17–0:22
After the blue car clears the intersection, show the yellow car going next.
The gray car waits.
Then the gray car begins to move only after the yellow car clears.

On-screen text:
“Then the next car goes.”

Voiceover:
“After the first car clears the intersection, the next car may go.”

SCENE 4 — SAME TIME ARRIVAL — 0:22–0:29
Reset the intersection.
Now show two cars only:
A blue car approaches from the bottom.
A red car approaches from the right side.
Both cars slow down and stop at the exact same time.

Freeze the frame for one second.
Add a clean highlight around both cars.

On-screen text:
“Both stopped at the same time.”

Voiceover:
“But what if two cars stop at the same time?”

SCENE 5 — CAR ON THE RIGHT GOES FIRST — 0:29–0:36
Switch to a clear overhead view.
Show the red car is on the RIGHT side of the blue car.
Place a curved arrow from the blue car toward the red car labeled:
“Car on your RIGHT”

On-screen text:
“Same time? The car on the RIGHT goes first.”

Voiceover:
“If two cars stop at the same time, the car on the right has the right of way.”

Show a green arrow above the red car.
The blue car waits.
The red car safely drives through the intersection first.

On-screen text:
“Red is on the right → Red goes first”

Voiceover:
“In this example, the red car is on the blue car’s right, so the red car goes first.”

SCENE 6 — OTHER CAR GOES AFTER — 0:36–0:40
After the red car clears the intersection, the blue car checks the road and drives straight through.

On-screen text:
“After the right-side car clears, you may go.”

Voiceover:
“Once the car on the right has gone and the intersection is clear, the other driver may proceed.”

ENDING CARD — 0:40–0:45
Show a simple clean diagram of a 4-way stop with four stop signs.

Final on-screen recap:
“4-Way Stop Rules”
“1. Stop completely.”
“2. First to stop goes first.”
“3. Same time? Right goes first.”

Voiceover:
“Remember: stop completely. First to stop goes first. Same time? The car on the right goes first.”

Important visual instructions:
- Make all STOP signs clearly red and readable.
- Make the white stop lines clear.
- Use only the cars needed for the lesson.
- Blue car is the main learner car.
- Red car demonstrates the car on the right.
- Use green arrows only for the car that has the right of way.
- Waiting cars should remain still.
- Keep all movement slow, safe, and realistic.
- Avoid confusing traffic, pedestrians, bicycles, emergency vehicles, or extra cars.
- The video must be simple enough for a beginner driver to understand immediately.

Negative prompt:
No accidents, no police, no sirens, no nighttime, no rain, no cluttered city traffic, no pedestrians crossing, no confusing road layouts, no cars rolling through stop signs, no unreadable signs, no aggressive driving.
```
