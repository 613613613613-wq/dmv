# Privacy Policy

*Effective date: at first App Store release. Last reviewed: 2026-04-28.*

## The short version

This app collects **nothing**. It does not have an account system. It does not contain analytics SDKs. It does not contain advertising SDKs. It does not communicate with our servers — we don't have any. Your study progress lives only on your iPhone.

This is the App Store privacy nutrition label this app ships under: **Data Not Collected**.

## What this means in detail

### What stays on your device

- Your answers, scores, and which questions you've gotten right or wrong
- Your bookmarks
- Your daily study streak
- Your settings (language preference, dyslexia-friendly font on/off, audio narration on/off)

All of this is stored in a local SQLite database in this app's own sandbox on your iPhone. It is not backed up to our servers because we don't have servers. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

### What does NOT happen

- We do not collect your name, email, or any account info — there is no account.
- We do not collect your location, device ID, advertising ID, or anything that could identify you.
- We do not contact any third-party server when you use the app — including ours. The app works in airplane mode for exactly this reason.
- We do not include analytics SDKs (no Firebase, no Mixpanel, no Amplitude, no Adjust, no Branch, nothing).
- We do not include ad SDKs.
- We do not use crash-reporting third parties; if the app crashes, the crash log is collected by Apple's built-in TestFlight / App Store crash logs only if you opted into sharing those with developers in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.
- We do not sell or share any data because there is no data leaving your device.

### What does require network

The only operation that uses the network is **App Store purchase / restore**. That happens through Apple's StoreKit, between your iPhone and Apple. We never see it; only Apple does. We can confirm a purchase exists; we cannot read your Apple ID or payment info.

### iCloud sync (future, opt-in)

A future version may offer iCloud sync of your study progress so you can study on iPad and iPhone with the same data. **It will be opt-in only.** When it ships, the toggle will be off by default and the data will sync via your private CloudKit container — meaning Apple delivers the bytes between your devices and we never see them. If we change the privacy posture in any way that affects this guarantee, we will update this policy and surface the change inside the app before turning anything on.

## Children

This app is appropriate for ages 13+ (Florida permit eligibility starts at 15). It does not collect data from anyone, including minors, because it does not collect data at all.

## Contact

If you have a privacy question, email us at support@example.com. We will reply, but most privacy questions are answered above by the simple fact that there is nothing to ask about — we don't have your data.

## Changes

If we ever change anything that affects this policy, we will:
1. Update the "Last reviewed" date at the top of this document.
2. Surface a notice in-app the next time you open it.
3. Update the App Store privacy nutrition label.
4. Ship the new policy *before* the change takes effect, never after.
