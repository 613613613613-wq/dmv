ABOUT THE EXAM

The Florida Class E knowledge test is 50 questions and you need 40 right (80%) to pass. As of February 2026 the exam itself is English-only — but you can study in Spanish or Haitian Creole inside the app, then take the official test in English.

First-time applicants must complete the 4-hour TLSAE course (Traffic Law and Substance Abuse Education) before the knowledge test. Under-18 applicants also need DETS, the 6-hour driver education course. We do not provide TLSAE or DETS — you complete those separately. We help you ace the knowledge exam.

QUESTIONS COME FROM THE HANDBOOK

Every question in this app traces back to a section in the official 2026 Florida Driver License Handbook published by FLHSMV. We don't scrape competitors; we don't make things up. If a question can't be linked to the handbook, it doesn't ship.
